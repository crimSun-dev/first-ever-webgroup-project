OUR FIRST DAY! OUR JOURNEY TO DONOMULYO VILLAGE IN MALANG!

On March 17, 2018, 72 grade 8 students of SMP Santa Laurensia were accompanied by 5 teachers went to Malang to attend Edutrip activities. They went to Terminal 3 of Soekarno Hatta Airport to meet before boarding an aircraft to Malang before 06.00. The trip to Malang is peaceful and when they arrive, they get off the plane and the weather is very sunny. They walked to Abdul Rachman Saleh Airport Malang and waited for their luggage in baggage claim. Then, they walk to the bus, the 8th graders are separated into 2 buses because there are many students and most students are resting because that day will be a long day. His students are so noisy in the bus that it is difficult to break. They took a break at Rumah Makan Kertasari for lunch. Most students are very tired because of the long trip from Jakarta to Malang. Their hunger has now replaced satiety and energized the activities in Donomulyo Village which was their last destination of the day. From the bus, the students enjoy the beautiful scenery because of the sunny weather and there are many plants around them. After three hours, the students arrived at Donomulyo Village.

But there was an accident with bus 2, the bus was stuck on the side of the road, the students panicked a bit on the accident. The teachers did not want any more trouble, they told the students to get off the bus and wait in front of him. There are students who are still shocked, some take photos and others just wait while talking with friends.

After the accident, the students of SMP Santa Laurensia waited for the truck to pick them up to drive them to the village. The road is bumpy and exciting because they have to stand in the truck and the journey is long enough. When they reached their destination, they were greeted by the villagers. They went into the chapel to rest and listen to the teachers' instructions. The students meet with their adoptive parents and one by one begin to go to their new home. For the rest of the day, the students perform activities with their adoptive parents till the night. According to Mrs. Jamilah (39 years old), a housewife, the majority of villagers work as farmers.
